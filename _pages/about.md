I am Dr. Omnia Ibrahim, a lecturer and researcher in Speech Technology, Phonetics, and Computational Linguistics. I am currently a Lecturer in the Department of Phonetics and Linguistics at Alexandria University

I obtained my PhD in Computational Linguistics and Phonetics from the University of Zürich, with additional doctoral training at Saarland University as part of the Integrated Research Training Group (IGK) within the Collaborative Research Center SFB 1102. Following my PhD, I worked as a postdoctoral researcher at Saarland University, contributing to large-scale interdisciplinary projects on information density and phonetic structure. I also serve as an Arabic linguistic consultant on international research projects related to literacy acquisition among adult migrants.

Alongside research, I have extensive teaching experience at both undergraduate and postgraduate levels, covering areas such as physiological, acoustic, auditory, and experimental phonetics, as well as speech science. I am deeply committed to student mentoring, academic service, and community engagement, and I have held several leadership and advisory roles within international research networks and student organizations.
